### AST
- **Chains are left-nested trees.** `c.center.d2()` is `MemberCallNode(object=FieldAccessNode(object=VarNameNode c, "center"), "d2")`. The parser never resolves types.
- `FieldAccessNode : ValueNode` has `std::unique_ptr<ExprNode> object`, `std::string field_name`, and `int field_index = -1` (checker fills it). It inherits `type`.
- `MemberCallNode : ValueNode` has `object`, `fn_name`, `args`, and `MemberFnDeclNode* fn = nullptr` (checker fills it). It inherits `type`.
- `object` is an `ExprNode` because the left side can be a variable, a field access or a call. No `StructDeclNode*` on these nodes: the struct is always found through `structs[object->type]`.

- `PrintNode` holds either one `ExprNode` or a string literal. `ReadNode` has no fields, and its type is always i32.
- `--ast` must dump every new node. Expected shapes: `MemberFn d2 -> i32`, `MemberCall d2` with a child `Field c.center`.
